The trail of one case

What the agent believed, what it chose to read, and what each step cost. Filled steps are beliefs. Open steps are actions. The NTSB's verdict appears only at the end, after the agent's answer.

This case is an invented example. The step kinds and limits are the real loop's: one belief before reading, up to two read choices, up to six coding calls, one answer.
